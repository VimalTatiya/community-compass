# First-timer preparation

## What do you wish someone had told you before you arrived in Las Vegas?

Plan for more walking than you expect, and build rest into every day. Distances between venues are longer than they look on a map.

## What should a first-time grantee complete, reserve or prepare before travelling to re:Invent?

Complete your registration, book travel and lodging early, and pick a few sessions with a backup for each time slot before you arrive. Download the event app and set up your schedule ahead of time.

## What should attendees pack specifically for re:Invent and the Las Vegas environment?

- Comfortable walking shoes
- A refillable water bottle, since the air is dry
- Sunscreen and sunglasses for time outdoors between venues
- A light layer, because indoor venues are air-conditioned
- A portable charger and cable
