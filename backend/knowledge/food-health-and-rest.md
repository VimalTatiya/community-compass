# Food, health and rest

## How did you manage meals, hydration, extensive walking, dry weather, long days and conference fatigue?

- Eat regular meals rather than skipping them to save time.
- Carry a water bottle and refill it often, since dry air makes dehydration easy to miss.
- Wear sunscreen outdoors, even on cloudy days.
- Plan one proper break in the middle of the day, and an early night.
- Rotate between two pairs of comfortable shoes if you can.

## Where can attendees find affordable food, quick meals, quiet spaces, charging points or places to rest near the conference venues?

Food courts, grab-and-go counters and coffee shops are usually the cheapest quick options. Check the official event map for lounges, rest areas and charging points. Hotel lobbies can be quieter than the main event floor.
