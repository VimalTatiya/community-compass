# Las Vegas outings

## Which Las Vegas activities or places would you recommend visiting after the conference day, particularly near the main re:Invent venues?

Walking part of the Strip in the evening is a classic option. Downtown's Fremont Street area is a short ride from the Strip and has a free canopy light show. Restaurants and cafes near the venues are easy choices after a long day.

## What would you recommend for someone with only 1–2 free hours, and what would you recommend for someone with half a day or a full free day?

- 1 to 2 hours: walk a stretch of the Strip or Fremont Street, or grab a meal near your hotel.
- Half a day: a scenic trip to Red Rock Canyon, booked as a tour or by rideshare. Allow time for travel both ways.
- Full day: a longer excursion, such as a guided tour to a nearby desert landscape or to Hoover Dam. Book in advance, since tours sell out.

## Which free, affordable, non-casino or solo-friendly Las Vegas experiences would you recommend to grant recipients? Please include approximate time, cost and transportation.

- Fremont Street Experience: walking and the light show are free. About 1 to 2 hours. Reach it by rideshare, taxi or the Deuce bus.
- Red Rock Canyon: a scenic drive and hiking area about 20 to 30 minutes west of the Strip. A per-vehicle fee applies. Allow about half a day. Use rideshare or a tour.
- Springs Preserve: a museum and botanical gardens with an entry fee. About 2 to 3 hours. Reach it by rideshare or bus.
- Neon Museum: a museum of old Las Vegas signs with an entry fee. About 1 to 2 hours. Reach it by rideshare or bus.
