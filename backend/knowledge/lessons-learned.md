# Lessons learned

## What was your biggest re:Invent mistake, your best decision and the single most important advice you would give next year's first-time grantees?

- Biggest mistake: overbooking the schedule and underestimating walking time between venues, which left me rushed and tired by midday.
- Best decision: picking one or two anchor sessions per day and keeping a backup for each slot.
- Most important advice: plan for rest, eat and drink regularly, and treat the conference as conversations as well as sessions.
