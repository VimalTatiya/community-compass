# Networking and community

## How did you meet useful contacts or make friends, and what advice would you give someone attending alone or feeling uncomfortable about networking?

Start with the people around you. Introduce yourself to the person next to you before or after a session, and ask what they are working on. Asking a good question in a session is an easy opener. Coming alone is common, and many people are looking for company too. If you feel uncomfortable, set a small goal, such as one new conversation a day, and step away when you need to recharge.

## Which official or unofficial community events, meetups, receptions, game nights or parties were worth attending? How did you discover them?

Check the official event app and site for receptions and meetups, and watch for announcements from community groups. Some events need a separate registration or invitation, so confirm that before you plan around them. Pick one or two that match your interests instead of trying to attend everything.
