# Venues and getting around

## Which re:Invent venues should not be scheduled back-to-back, and how much travel time should attendees allow between them?

Avoid back-to-back sessions at venues that are far apart on the official map. Allow at least 20 to 30 minutes between venues, and more during peak times when crowds slow movement.

## What practical advice can you share about walking, shuttles and the Las Vegas Monorail between The Venetian, Wynn/Encore, Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace and MGM Grand?

- Walk when the route is short and the weather is comfortable, and take shuttles for longer legs.
- The Monorail does not serve every venue, so check its route map before relying on it.
- Rideshares can be slow at peak times, so do not count on them for a tight schedule.
- Check the event's transport notices each day for shuttle schedules and stops.

## Which venue entrances, internal routes, shuttle stops or navigation details were confusing during your visit?

Large casino complexes have long indoor corridors and several entrances, so the one on your map may not be the closest to you. Look for conference signage, ask staff, and allow extra time to find the right room. Confirm the shuttle stop before you leave, since stops can differ by day.
