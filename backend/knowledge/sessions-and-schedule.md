# Sessions and schedule

## How did you select sessions, and which formats (workshops, chalk talks, builder sessions, breakouts, labs, code talks or keynotes) provided the most value?

Start with your goal for the week, then pick one or two must-attend sessions per day and fill the rest with options. Hands-on formats such as workshops, builder sessions and labs work best when you want to build something. Chalk talks and code talks suit questions and discussion. Breakouts give broad overviews, and keynotes cover announcements.

## How many sessions can someone realistically attend in one day without becoming exhausted?

Three or four sessions a day is a reasonable target for most people. Hands-on workshops are more tiring than talks, so count them as heavier.

## How should a first-time attendee balance sessions, keynotes, Expo visits, meals, networking, travel and rest? Please describe your recommended daily schedule.

- Morning: a light breakfast, then one or two sessions
- Midday: a proper lunch and a 30 to 60 minute break away from the sessions
- Afternoon: one or two sessions, plus Expo time if you want it
- Late afternoon: rest, or walk back to your hotel
- Evening: an optional reception, meetup or dinner

## Which sessions should attendees prioritize in person, and which can usually be watched later as recordings?

Prioritize workshops, builder sessions and chalk talks in person, where questions and hands-on practice matter most. Breakouts and keynotes are more often recorded. Check the catalog for each session before you skip it, since recordings are not guaranteed.

## What should an attendee do when a session is full, cancelled, overlaps with another session or is too far away?

- Full: join the waitlist if one is offered, and check whether walk-up seats are allowed.
- Cancelled: check the event app for notices, then move to the backup you chose in advance.
- Overlaps: pick the session that matches your goal most closely, and check whether the other one is recorded.
- Too far away: choose an alternative in a nearby venue, or leave time to walk or take transport.

## Which conference features beyond scheduled sessions were most valuable (for example Ask the Experts, Content Hubs, community spaces, lounges, Expo activities or certification programmes)?

Expo and hands-on zones are useful for quick questions to vendors and for trying tools. Ask-the-experts desks can save time on specific problems. Community spaces and lounges are good for meeting people and resting between sessions.
